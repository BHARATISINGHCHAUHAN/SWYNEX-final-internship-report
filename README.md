# \## 🔍 Key Business Insights

# 

# The analysis highlighted the following findings:

# 

# \- 🌍 \*\*Geography:\*\* Germany recorded the highest churn rate at approximately 32%, compared with France at 16% and Spain at 17%.

# \- 👩 \*\*Gender:\*\* Female customers showed a higher churn rate of approximately 25%, compared with 16% for male customers.

# \- 👵 \*\*Age Group:\*\* Senior customers had the highest churn rate at approximately 45%, followed by middle-aged customers at 20%. Young customers had the lowest rate at approximately 8%.

# \- 💳 \*\*Credit Score:\*\* Customers with lower credit scores showed relatively higher churn.

# \- 🏦 \*\*Account Balance:\*\* Higher-balance customers showed churn rates of approximately 23%, indicating a need to focus on retaining valuable accounts.

# \- 📦 \*\*Number of Products:\*\* Customers holding three or four products showed very high churn rates. These groups should be investigated carefully because their customer counts were small.

# \- 💰 \*\*Salary:\*\* Churn rates were relatively similar across salary groups, at approximately 20%–21%.

# \- 📅 \*\*Tenure:\*\* Churn varied across tenure groups, with some shorter-tenure groups showing relatively higher churn.

# 

# \*Note: Figures are approximate and based on the findings documented in the project report.\*

# 

# \## 💡 Business Recommendations

# 

# Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve customer retention:

# 

# 1\. \*\*Focus on Germany:\*\* Investigate customer service, pricing, and competition in the highest-churn geography.

# 2\. \*\*Retain Senior Customers:\*\* Introduce personalized services and targeted retention programs.

# 3\. \*\*Improve Female Customer Retention:\*\* Understand customer needs and develop more relevant products and communication strategies.

# 4\. \*\*Protect High-Value Accounts:\*\* Offer loyalty benefits and personalized relationship management to high-balance customers.

# 5\. \*\*Review Multi-Product Customers:\*\* Investigate why customers with three or four products are leaving.

# 6\. \*\*Monitor Customer Churn:\*\* Use the interactive dashboard regularly to track changes in churn rates and evaluate retention initiatives.
